Dictionary v2.5: hand-drawn vocabulary illustrations (20 words)

Added 20 illustrations from a photographed hand-drawn vocabulary sheet:
baba, mama, kitabu, mwalimu, chakula, kula, mti, maji, nyumba,
kukimbia, furaha, huzuni, amani, afya ya akili, afya ya mama,
afya ya mtandao, afya ya uzazi, afya ya wanyama, agaa, agano jipya.

Processing:
- Cropped individual cards from the source sheet (5x4 grid).
- Removed baked-in text labels/numbers so the app renders its own
  typography (consistent theming, accessibility).
- Trimmed row bleed artifacts (top/bottom edges from adjacent cards).
- Upscaled 2x with LANCZOS and saved as WebP (quality 88).
- Bundled as `res/drawable/word_<headword>.webp` (20 files, ~380 KB total).

DB schema bumped to version 5: added `image_res` column mapping
headword -> drawable resource name. 20 entries populated.

UI:
- Search results: words with an illustration show a small 🖼 badge
  (colored circle) next to the headword -- non-intrusive, doesn't
  affect row height for the other 16,136 entries.
- Detail screen: illustration displayed prominently between the
  part-of-speech line and the definition, 200dp tall, rounded corners.

Also fixed the "has illustration" indicator to use a simple badge
instead of a Material Icon (avoids the `material-icons-extended`
coverage gap for illustration/photo icons).

Updated THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and README.md with the illustration
methodology. Backup CSV/XLSX already reflects the image_res mapping.
