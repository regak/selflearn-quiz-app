Feature: Swahili dictionary for chapter vocabulary

Add a per-chapter English-Swahili glossary and an in-app dictionary
lookup so students more comfortable in Swahili can look up unfamiliar
English vocabulary used in the chapter text without leaving the
reading or quiz screen.

- data/ContentRepository.kt: new GlossaryTerm(term, englishDefinition,
  swahili) data class; Chapter gains a `glossary` list. Authored 10
  key-vocabulary entries for each chapter (Rivers and Erosion; The
  Water Cycle).
- ui/GlossaryDialog.kt (new): "Swahili Dictionary" shown as a Dialog
  overlay (not a nav route) with a live search box filtering by
  English term, English definition, or Swahili text. Overlay keeps the
  underlying screen's composition (and its state/timers) alive.
- ui/ReadingScreen.kt: translate-icon button in the top bar opens the
  glossary without affecting the countdown timer.
- ui/QuizScreen.kt: translate-icon button in the quiz header opens the
  glossary without affecting hearts/XP/streak/current question state.

Caveat noted in README: the Swahili translations are a best-effort
starting point, not a professionally verified translation -- flagged
for review by a Swahili speaker before real student use.
