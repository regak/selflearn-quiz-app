SelfLearn Quiz App -- Prototype v1.0

Gamified "read a chapter, then get quizzed" self-learning app prototype
for English-language content (Swahili planned later).

What's included:
- Home screen with 2 hardcoded chapters: Geography "Rivers and Erosion"
  and Science "The Water Cycle" (~350-450 words each).
- Settable reading timer (default 5 min) before each chapter.
- Reading screen: Start Quiz unlocks when the timer hits zero OR the
  student scrolls to the bottom of the text, whichever happens first.
- Quiz screen: opening Socratic free-text question with model-answer
  self-assessment (Not quite / Sort of / Got it!), followed by
  multiple-choice and true/false questions of increasing difficulty
  with instant feedback. 3 hearts, XP, and streak bonus game mechanics.
- Results screen with score, XP, best streak, encouraging message, and
  an expandable "Review missed questions" list.

Build: Kotlin + Jetpack Compose + Material3, min SDK 24, arm64-v8a debug
build. Built headlessly with gradle 8.9 / JDK 17 / Android SDK 34, no
Android Studio.

Install: download app-debug.apk on your Android phone and open it from
your Downloads / browser to sideload (allow "install from unknown
sources" if prompted). This is a debug prototype build, not signed for
Play Store distribution.
