Dictionary v2.4: real sentences for 60% of words + QA cleanup

Per feedback: the previous generated fallback copied the definition
back as a sentence (not a real usage example); revise to use real
examples where possible, with an improved generated fallback for the
rest.

Expanded real-sentence coverage from 14.5% to ~60% by adding Swahili
Wikipedia (sw.wikipedia.org, 131,547 articles, CC BY-SA 4.0) as a
third real-example source, matched against headwords by whole-word
(single-word headwords) and substring (multi-word headwords) search,
screened for sentence length (prefer shorter/simpler) and a basic
sensitive-topic keyword filter.

Rewrote the generated fallback templates entirely: the old ones
("Neno X linamaanisha: {definition}.") just restated the definition,
which isn't a usage example at all. New templates place the headword
in a plausible classroom/reading/conversation SCENARIO instead (e.g.
'Mwalimu alitumia neno "{hw}" wakati wa somo la Kiswahili leo.'),
POS-agnostic so they stay grammatically safe without needing to
conjugate around the headword.

QA pass after the first Wikipedia-matching build caught 138 spurious
"real" matches that needed reverting back to generated:
- 5 were bound-morpheme headwords (e.g. "-kubwa", "-mji", "-zima") --
  grammatical roots that are never standalone words, so any text
  "match" for them was an accidental hyphen/punctuation artifact, not
  genuine usage.
- 134 still had leftover MediaWiki markup fragments (stray "* *"
  bullet points, "]]" image-caption remnants) that slipped through the
  plain-text extraction's cleanup regex.

Final: 9,676 real (59.9%), 6,480 generated (40.1%). Bumped
PRAGMA user_version to 4 and REQUIRED_SCHEMA_VERSION accordingly so
existing installs correctly re-copy the updated database.

Updated swahili_dictionary_backup.csv/.xlsx and
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md / README.md with the corrected methodology and
counts, including the Wikipedia CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution.
