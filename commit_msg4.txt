Add a prominent Swahili Dictionary button on the Home screen

Per feedback: the dictionary needed its own standalone, visible button
(not only tucked into the Reading/Quiz screens). Add a "Kamusi"
(Dictionary) button to the Home screen top bar, next to the app title,
that opens a combined Swahili dictionary covering every chapter's
glossary at once -- so a student can look up a word before even
picking a chapter.

- ui/GlossaryDialog.kt: generalized from `GlossaryDialog(chapter, ...)`
  to `GlossaryDialog(title, subtitle, terms, onDismiss)` so the same
  dialog can show either one chapter's glossary or the merged
  all-chapters list.
- ui/HomeScreen.kt: new dictionary button (icon + "Kamusi" label) in
  the top bar; opens GlossaryDialog with every chapter's terms merged.
- ui/ReadingScreen.kt, ui/QuizScreen.kt: updated to the new
  GlossaryDialog signature (still show just that chapter's glossary).
